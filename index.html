ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Apply?

Eligibility depends on the specific grant program. Applicants should provide truthful information and review the requirements for the opportunity they are applying for.

  • Meet the published eligibility requirements.
  • Provide accurate contact and project information.
  • Explain how the requested support will be used.
  • Submit the application before the stated deadline.

Important

Submitting an application does not guarantee funding. Do not pay anyone who claims they can guarantee approval.
